Shows & Sports in Charlotte

Charlotte has plenty of entertainment options, from watching a Hornets game to seeing your favorite artist perform live. Explore events and activities to enjoy with friends.

Explore Entertainment Options

Charlotte Hornets Games

Watch the Charlotte Hornets play at Spectrum Center. It's a fun option for basketball fans visiting the city during the NBA season.

You can check out the Spectrum Center Charlotte website to view upcoming basketball games schedules and tickets.

Concerts and Live Music

Check out concerts and live performances at venues around Charlotte, including the Fillmore.

You can find shows on the Official Fillmore site or explore more events on the Spectrum Center Charlotte website.

Other Events

Look for festivals, special events, and other activities happening during your visit. Charlotte offers more than concerts and sports. Visitors can enjoy roller coasters at Carowinds, outdoor adventures at the U.S. National Whitewater center. These activities offer different ways to enjoy the city with friends or family.
